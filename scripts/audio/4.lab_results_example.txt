================================================================================
SUGGESTED ADDITIONAL FIELDS FOR DENTAL WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

The following fields would enhance this workflow:

1. specimen_type (string)
   - Example: "Oral tissue biopsy", "Saliva sample", "Gum swab"
   - Helps AI agent describe what test was performed

2. site_location (string)
   - Example: "Lower left gum near molar #19", "Upper palate"
   - Helps patient confirm correct test is being discussed

3. lab_name (string)
   - Example: "Pacific Oral Pathology Lab"
   - Patient may want to know who processed their specimen

4. follow_up_appointment_date (date)
   - Example: "12/20/2025"
   - If provider review is needed, when patient should come in

5. urgent_flag (boolean)
   - Indicates if results require immediate provider attention
   - Changes callback_timeframe and call handling

6. patient_consent_verified (boolean)
   - HIPAA compliance - confirms patient authorized to receive results
   - Some results may require in-person disclosure

7. result_category (string)
   - Example: "Normal", "Abnormal - Benign", "Abnormal - Requires Follow-up"
   - Standardized categories for reporting
